I am a weird guy.
Nobody wants to look twice.
Nobody cares enough to see through me.
Nobody loves me the way I am.
Still, in me, there is a guy
who claps on strangers' birthdays.
Who buys flowers for strangers on impulse.
Who motivates others
just because he sees potential in them.
And yes.
I am the guy who is just weird.
And that's fine.
