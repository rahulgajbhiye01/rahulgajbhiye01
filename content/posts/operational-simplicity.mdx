---
title: Operational simplicity is a feature
description: Why fewer moving parts often create a more resilient platform.
tags:
  - Operations
  - Reliability
  - Architecture
date: 2026-05-26
---

Every service, queue, dashboard, and automation has an operational cost. That cost is easy to miss when a system is new because the happy path is usually simple. It appears later, when a team needs to debug a failure at an inconvenient hour.

Simplicity does not mean avoiding useful tools. It means choosing the smallest set of components that makes ownership, recovery, and change understandable. A boring dependency with strong operational knowledge is often better than a more capable tool that no one is prepared to support.

Before adding a component, ask who will operate it, how it fails, and how it will be removed.
