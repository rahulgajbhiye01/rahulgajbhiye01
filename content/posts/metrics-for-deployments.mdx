---
title: The metrics that matter during a deployment
description: A focused set of signals for deciding whether a rollout is healthy.
tags: [Observability, Deployments, SRE]
date: 2026-04-08
---

Deployment dashboards work best when they answer one question: should this rollout continue? Start with user-facing error rate and latency, then add saturation signals for the dependency most likely to constrain the service.

Compare the new version with a stable baseline and keep the time window short. A delayed aggregate can make a failing rollout appear healthy long enough to increase the blast radius.

Finally, document the rollback decision. Teams should not need to debate which signal matters while customers are affected.
